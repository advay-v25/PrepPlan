# Roadmap — Founder Context Document

Everything Advay has stated about this product, reconstructed from the full project transcript
(31 Aug – 4 Sep 2026). Direct quotes are marked. Nothing here is invented; where his statements
conflict with each other, the conflict is recorded rather than resolved.

---

## 1. The origin and the problem

The product exists because of a personal experience with CFA:

> "For CFA, the main issue i had was that there was no structure for me to follow, or for me to
> understand how things link. As i dove deeper into the subject across the months, i started to
> understand how it all linked together closer to my exam."

The insight is that the linkage between topics arrives too late — students only understand how the
curriculum connects in the final weeks, when it is least useful. The product's job is to make that
structure visible from week one.

The primary user population:

> "The main reason for this app is for students in India who havent ever given a foreign exam and
> dont understand the rigours and the mental requirements always struggle to adapt and this platform
> can help them build structure."

The purchase motive is emotional, not informational:

> "They pay me because of their anxiousness, confusion, unstructuredness leading to procrastination."

This is the load-bearing sentence for the whole product. The user is not buying content or
teaching. They are buying relief from not knowing what to do next.

---

## 2. What the product is — and is not

**It is:**

> "This is a supercharged, competitive exam oriented study planner with tests, accountiblity
> mechanisms, trackers and dashboards and anything else to boost productivity and time management."

> "We would be majorly helping planning, structuring and keeping you consistent with proper
> accountablity. You need to do things and put in efforts to see results. We want to draw a roadmap
> for that."

> "This isnt about teaching people about their course, its about showing them the right way to
> learn, the path to achieve their goals and teaching them accountability and tracking their
> studies."

**It is not:**

- Not a teaching app. *"I need you to draft a guidance app rather than a teaching app."*
- Not a content/prep provider. *"We are not selling CFA material here."*
- Not an all-in-one study helper. *"This is not an all in one study help kind of thing."*
- Not a competitor to Schweser or CFAI. *"we cant teach them FSA from start, or an entire subject.
  That will take us competing with a prep provider."*
- Not a clone or fork of PlannrAI (see §9).

**The teaching boundary, stated precisely:**

> "We can only guide them on learning specific things for example if they get inventories questions
> wrong, then we would point toward FIFO vs. LIFO points, Risk and Return questions point toward
> specific places they messed up, not teaching them what is FIFO and what is LIFO."

The app names the gap. It does not fill the gap. Filling it is the student's work, done in their own
material.

---

## 3. Scope

**v1 (now):** CFA Level I only, 2027 curriculum. Schweser 2027 is the default reference —
*"2027 scwesser is the first bible unless someone uploads their material."* It is used as a
structural/mapping reference, never reproduced: *"It wont be mentioned or anything just used as
reference."*

**Long-term exam ladder**, in his stated order:

1. CA, CFA, FRM, ACCA, CMA, CS
2. Pre-MBA — CAT, CET, etc.
3. CLAT
4. CUET, CET
5. GRE, GMAT, GATE

**Mapping precision, decided:** pointers into the reference must be *"precise to the LOS"* — not
book, not reading, not page. LOS-level.

**Build constraints:** solo build, *"the timeline to perfect this is till Jan."*

---

## 4. The core loop

Stated across two messages, one for each student type.

**The coaching student:**

> "suppose i have my CFA class on saturday and sunday/ monday and wednesday and friday, I wll load
> what i was taught in those 2 classes into the app, and then the schedule of what to do should be
> generated by the app. It should tell me what exactly i have to study, important not to miss points
> of remembrance, how to make a train of thought for something, real life application, etc."

> "after a lecture or block, there needs to be diagnostic test which is done by the user according
> to which his revision plan for the week will be made."

**The unenrolled self-studier:**

> "we will first figure out the person's strong subjects and points through a 5-7 questions, draft
> their study plan till a tentative exam date if they havent chosen or if they have then till that
> point in time."

**The verification principle:**

> "The chapter wise or module wise diagnostic tests that are given are the verification source."

> "every single thing that is studied by the user should be looked to be applied through questions,
> case studies, etc. in the app and then judged and graded accordingly. A preparedness score would
> go off of that."

Self-report never moves readiness. Only diagnostic evidence does.

**The weekly replan input set:**

> "after the first week it would be based on their completion of the first week, their actual and
> missed vs total planned, diagnostic test scores, confidence level and anything else we could
> analyse to give us a more complete picture of their overall work in week 1."

## 5. Diagnostics — the differentiator

> "The diagnostic tests are our special sauce. I have trained GPT into framing proper diagnostic
> tests with language and everything."

> "mocks for CFA should be the style and benchmark of questions."

The AI coach is scoped to what the diagnostics reveal: *"The AI coach should handle weak points,
concept misses, etc."* Supporting surfaces named: *"There will be explanation based checks, a
subject sheet that tracks your scores, misses, strong points, etc."*

Grading approach chosen: **explanation-based checks plus score import.**

Diagnostics also serve a psychological function, not only a measurement one:

> "We will test, build accountability and help the users get over their fear of tests and questions
> through constant diagnostic tests."

And the accountability mechanism is not separate from them:

> "The weekly commitment ritual are a diagnostic tests."

## 6. Time and hours — the hardest constraint

He rejected the industry-standard fixed-hours assumption outright:

> "CFA does not take 300 hours. For different types of individuals, it takes different amounts of
> time. For a college student who has no prior knowledge of finance, the time requirement could
> double or even in some extreme cases triple. I am not sure how to program this, because different
> individuals could require different times. We need to keep it such that the time requirement is
> dynamic and it can change based on how the user progresses with their tests, completions, etc."

> "Lets keep the lower limit to 350 hours and the upper limit of this to 1000 hours overall."

> "I have seen people pass CFA in 3 months and also in a year. you can never figure out how and what
> is what. Dont decide their hours of study."

> "There are multiple things in real life that are different from the theoritical study plan. We
> need to account for deviations, actual capacity of the person studying and anything else to give
> us the best measure."

**Bootstrap approach:**

> "We can take the first week to diagnose their required hours and then adjust accordingly.
> According to the onboarding choices we can set their first week plan and then work accordingly."

**Planning granularity:**

> "You have to plan things week by week for them to understand what to do and how to do things."

**Sequencing is not fixed:**

> "Week 1, 2 and 3 could be all quant and week 4,5,6,7 and 8 could all be FSA and so on if the
> person is learning from scratch or doing their first revision or whatever. This is just an
> example. the algorithm needs to account for all of that."

## 7. Access philosophy

The most forceful instruction in the project, and the one that overturned prior work:

> "We cannot deny someone access. thats not how a software is sold. Your role is to build a
> software, not be god and decide who gets access and who does not. We are to help, not decide."

> "You need to just get information about the user. The planning will be done as the user
> progresses."

> "I want you to prepare the onboarding flow in such a manner that the software can get maximum
> context about the user and prepare the tools accordingly. We are only to plan each week and help
> them organise their studies. Nothing else."

**⚠️ This contradicts an earlier decision.** On the ONBOARDING-brainstorm question *"Does the Tier 0
check need a hard gate on obviously infeasible cases, or do we let a candidate with 3 weeks proceed
anyway with a triage plan?"* he answered **"Gating"**. The later instruction is unambiguous and
supersedes it — the hard gate has been retired (D12) — but the earlier reasoning has never been
addressed. If a user with three weeks left signs up, the product currently says nothing about
feasibility at all. That is a swing from one extreme to the other, and the honest middle (tell them
the truth, then let them in anyway) has not been designed.

## 9. Hard constraints

**PlannrAI — reference only, zero reuse:**

> "We cannot use anything from PlannrAI. We will need to create everything from scratch personalised
> to our app. We cannot copy or take anything from there. We can use it as reference and have to
> build everything by ourselves. No calendar copying or coach script copying. This entire thing will
> be built from scratch and different working to what plannrAI is."

**Copyright:** Schweser 2027 is a mapping reference only. *"modify the content in that to which we
dont get sued but we can help the user with only specifics rather than a detailed teaching lesson."*
Imported user material stays account-scoped and never enters a shared question bank.

**The 617 GB corpus:** originally proposed as a paid add-on to give the AI more context —
*"an option to buy our 617Gb worth of data on CFA L1."* Sourced from *"Google Drive, combining
multiple drive links that i receive and basically making documents on them."* Its status is now
unclear; it has not been mentioned since the earliest exchange, and its provenance is a copyright
exposure that has never been assessed.

**Commercial:** the roadmap itself is **paid**, not a free acquisition tier.

**Both user types supported from day one:** *"I am targeting both kinds of people, who can use their
notes to run this class by class and those who need a structure from day 1."*

## 10. How he wants to be worked with

> "I want you to help me ideate, develop and tell me where what can be done practically always
> thinking from a mass user perspective. Wherever i am being linear, you have to be multi
> directional."

Everything is judged against mass-user viability, not the ideal single user. He asks to be
contradicted when his thinking is single-track. Phased delivery, one phase settled before the next
opens.

## 12. Open, unresolved

1. **The gating contradiction (§7).** Never reconciled. What does the product say to someone with
   three weeks and no preparation?
2. **What replaces the 617 GB corpus?** He asked directly — *"If we dont use the 617GB data then
   what do we do?"* — and the question was never answered. The AI's context source is currently
   "the CFA L1 curriculum," which is not a corpus.
3. **The full feature graph.** Requested and still outstanding: *"lets discuss the product flow, how
   a user would use each and every feature and lets create a full graph of every single feature and
   how it is useful to the user."*
4. **Week two.** Every stated principle is about the weekly loop; nothing built or designed so far
   produces a second week.
5. **Flashcards, notes, reading material.** In the opening vision, conditional on copyright since,
   never scoped.
6. **The 5–7 question intake for self-studiers** (§4) has no current implementation and no
   relationship to the diagnostic engine.
7. **Deviation handling.** *"account for deviations, actual capacity"* is the core promise and has no
   mechanism — nothing currently reads what actually happened last week.

---

## 13. The one-line test

Everything shipped should pass this: **does it reduce the anxiety of not knowing what to do next
week, using evidence of what the student actually did and actually knows?**

If a feature teaches, prescribes hours, denies access, or reports progress without diagnostic
evidence behind it, it fails his stated intent.
