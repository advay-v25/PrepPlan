Roadmap is a CFA Level I planning and accountability product. It helps students decide how and when to work. Study instructions describe the activity, time, completion and next check. The Coach stays within planning, weak-area triage and study methods. Keep setup mandatory for verified users, with no feasibility barrier. Honor protected time and sitting limits. Self-study work runs Monday through Saturday, with the weekly assessment on Sunday. For class-led candidates, confirmed class boundaries drive cycles and the assessment precedes the closing class. A calendar display week and a study cycle can have different boundaries.

## 2. Verify planning across the whole journey

| Student and deviation | What must happen |

| Three subjects partly or wholly covered, shaky on two | Carry reported coverage and uncertainty into the first accepted week. Schedule unfinished and uncertain scope differently. Offer supported calibration where there are enough items. Completed work stays attributed to its original cycle. Reassess only what evidence warrants. |
| First pass complete, mock in a month | Begin with revision and selective calibration. Use recall, timed mixed practice where eligible, and mock preparation. No per-reading diagnostic marathon. Preserve the booked mock and schedule debrief, weak-area repairs and spaced checks around the student's commitments. |

| Packed week, missed work, late assessment | Keep reported minutes separate from planned minutes. Explain what fits, what waits and what is carried forward. | Ahead of plan, moved exam, last week, exam sat | Rebase remaining milestones from confirmed progress, keep revision and mock intent, and show calm choices when the desired scope exceeds capacity.
